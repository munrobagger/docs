# Community Guidelines

Welcome to the Munro Bagger community! These guidelines help us maintain a safe, supportive, and inspiring space for all baggers.

## Content Standards

### ✅ DO Share

- Photos from your Munro Bagging adventures
- Accurate route descriptions and conditions
- Helpful tips and advice
- Achievements and milestones
- Encouraging comments and support

### ❌ DON'T Share

- Offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content
- False or misleading information about routes or conditions
- Content that bullies, harasses, or threatens others
- Spam, advertisements, or promotional material
- Private information about other users
- Content that encourages dangerous behavior
- Nudity, violence, or graphic content
- Copyright-protected material without permission

## Safety Information

When sharing route information or mountain conditions:
- Be accurate and include the date
- Note any hazards or difficulties
- Don't encourage risky behavior
- Remember that conditions change rapidly
- Your information could affect someone's safety decisions

**Disclaimer**: All user-generated content is provided by community members. Always verify information independently and make your own safety assessments.

## Content Moderation

We use several methods to keep our community safe:
- Community reporting system
- Manual review of reported content
- Regular monitoring of community activity

Content that violates these guidelines will be removed, and offending users may be banned.

## How to Report Content

If you see something that doesn't belong:

1. **In-App Reporting**
   - Tap the three dots (...) on any post or profile
   - Select "Report"
   - Submit

2. **Email Reporting**
   - For urgent safety concerns: hello@munrobagger.scot

**We aim to review all reports within 24 hours**

## Blocking Users

To block someone who is bothering you:
1. Visit their profile
2. Tap the three dots (...) menu
3. Select "Block User"

When you block someone:
- They can't see your profile
- They can't on your content
- You won't see their content

## Updates to Guidelines

We may update these guidelines as our community grows or as we add new features.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please get in touch via email at hello@munrobagger.scot.

## Remember

The mountains are challenging enough without negativity in our community. Be kind, be helpful, and enjoy sharing your passion for Munro bagging with others who understand the magic of Scotland's peaks.

Happy bagging! 🏔️
